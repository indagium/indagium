# Indagium 1.8.12

Indagium 1.8.12 makes writing and refining investigation notes faster with shared dictation, local English suggestions, and AI-powered note rewriting.

## Dictation and writing assistance

Use the same microphone control wherever you write prose: notes, the AI composer, AI test prompts, and other multi-line editors. Right-click the mic to select the spoken language and optional translation. Recording status remains visible in note footers, and silent microphone input now explains when macOS microphone permission needs attention instead of producing invented text.

English text can now show spelling and grammar suggestions directly in the editor. Misspellings use a red wavy underline and grammar suggestions an amber dotted underline; open suggestions by hovering, clicking, or pressing `Alt+Enter`. Suggestions remain stable while you type, and dictated sentences capitalize naturally at their start.

## Notes and evidence

Use **Rewrite with AI** to refine a saved note without losing its intent. Captured log excerpts now preserve their displayed row labels, elapsed time, process details, and presentation choices at the time you add them, so later view or copy-setting changes cannot alter the evidence in an existing note. Toggle the available `Δt`, `Row #`, `PID/TID`, and `Process` chips directly in an excerpt header.

The Notes panel no longer shifts its scroll position when you focus an empty editor, and suggestion-pill hover effects stay clipped to their rounded shape.

## Fixes

- Dictation no longer becomes unavailable after a model-verification attempt.
- Voice recording and editor replacement no longer risk freezing when recognition finishes at the same time.
