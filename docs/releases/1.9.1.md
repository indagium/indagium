# Indagium 1.9.1

Indagium 1.9.1 adds special themes, tab groups, reusable workspace layouts, and more precise sequence diagram editing.

- Give your workspace a new look with five special themes, each available in light and dark.
- Keep related logs and diagrams together in named, colored tab groups.
- Save and share log layouts, and choose the layout, appearance and editor defaults through a workspace profile.
- Move individual diagram arrows, edit PlantUML alongside the canvas, and customize notes and groups.

## Special themes

**Settings › Appearance › Theme › Special themes** offers **Pumpkin patch**, **Haunted manor**, **Trick or treat**, **Beer time · Yellow** and **Beer time · Green**. Each has light and dark palettes, a scene behind the top tab strip, picture icons and animated decorations.

Choose **Always**, or let Automatic turn themes on during their schedule. The Halloween themes run October 24–November 1; Beer time runs on Fridays from 17:00, using your local clock. Outside an automatic theme's schedule, another theme whose schedule is active can appear.

Set effects to **Full**, **Calm** or **Off**. Calm reduces motion and keeps effects away from the log; Off keeps static decorations. Effects can pause while capturing or playing video. The **Layout** menu offers individual decoration switches. Selecting a regular theme turns the special theme off.

The new-theme popup lets you preview each light or dark version before choosing **Use theme**, or **Skip** to keep your current appearance.

## Tab groups

Group related log and diagram tabs from a tab's context menu. Give each group a name, color and optional icon, and choose whether its color tints the member tabs.

Drag a group to move its tabs together, or move a tab into another group. A log's open diagrams follow it when it joins, moves between or leaves groups. Collapsing a group keeps its active tab visible; group membership and order are restored on the next launch.

**Settings › Appearance › Tabs** also lets you put close buttons on the left or right.

## Layouts and workspace profiles

The **Layout** menu offers **Classic**, **Log focus**, **Filter bar**, **Filtering** and **Analysis** presets for a single log view. Save your own arrangement with its panel visibility, tile sizes, collapsed sections and lock setting. Rename, update, revert, export or import a saved layout. A small dot on the Layout button indicates that the current layout has been modified.

Workspace profiles in **Settings › General** combine appearance and editor behavior with a preset or saved layout. Custom profiles can also **Keep current layout**. Exporting a profile includes its referenced saved layout so it can be imported elsewhere.

Applying a profile restores its appearance and editor defaults. Older profile files use app defaults for fields they do not contain and keep the current layout when they have no layout reference. The setup assistant saves a custom **My setup** profile when you customize its choices.

## Sequence diagrams

Switch the Messages tile between **Templates** for editing shared message definitions and **Sequence** for one row per drawn arrow. Reorder individual arrows by dragging in the list or canvas, using the **Move** menu, or pressing **Alt+↑ / Alt+↓**. Reset a custom order to return to chronological order.

Use **Edit** in the diagram toolbar to open a **PlantUML** tile beside the canvas. Edit the source and press **Apply** to update the diagram in one undo step. Invalid edits show diagnostics; a draft is preserved if the diagram changes and must be reloaded before applying. Removing marked log evidence requires confirmation.

Text editors stay readable at any zoom. Renaming a repeated message shows how many arrows share its label and offers **Rename only this arrow**. Add a custom message or selected log rows after a specific arrow, resize lifeline headers, and choose fill, border and text colors for notes and groups. Notes wrap their full text on the canvas and in PNG exports.

See the [sequence diagram walkthrough](https://indagium.com/sequence-diagrams/) for the diagram workflow.
