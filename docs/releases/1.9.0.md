# Indagium 1.9.0

Indagium 1.9.0 turns the whole app into movable, resizable tiles: the log view, compare, the New tab, the diagram workspace and the Tests workspace. The tab strip and toolbar can dock to any window edge, and official builds now come with an end user license agreement that you accept once, the first time you start this version.

## The log view as tiles

Every part of the log view is now a tile: each filter section, Original, Log, Notes, the diagram library, AI and Video. Drag a tile by its grip to move it, drag the gaps between tiles to resize them, and collapse, hide or maximize any tile. The **Layout** button next to Settings offers presets (Classic, Review, Log focus, Filters on top), a checklist of panels, Lock tiles and Reset. Your existing panel widths carry over, and the layout is shared by every tab.

- Side sections stack in columns that scroll, like the old sidebar. Each section is as tall as its content by default, and a section with a list can be made as tall as you like.
- Lists use one thin scrollbar each, and the Notes actions sit in the Notes tile's header.
- A narrow Log tile switches its toolbar to icons and then a "⋯" menu, and drops the PID/TID columns. Copying a row still includes them.
- With a log tile maximized, Ctrl/Cmd+F searches the tile on screen.

## Compare as four tiles

Compare shows Original A, Original B, Log A and Log B as separate tiles next to the filter, notes and AI tiles, with its own presets (Compare grid and rows). A strip above them chooses whether filters apply to **A**, **B** or **Both**; with A only, A's highlighters still paint B. The same strip can link both logs' scrolling by time.

## Tabs and toolbar

- **Settings › Appearance › Tabs** offers eight tab styles, fixed or fit-to-label widths, label forms (including "File — folder") and options for file-type badges, source stripes and close buttons. The default look is unchanged.
- The tab strip can sit on any window edge, and a vertical tab bar can be resized.
- The toolbar can sit before or after the tabs, on its own edge, or inside the log view as a tile. Drag either one by its grip onto a window edge, or lock them in place.

## New tab

The New tab's Open, Capture and Run tests sections are tiles you can rearrange, resize, hide and restore from presets.

- **Open** keeps its large tiles while there is room, then switches to compact buttons. A splitter divides the actions from recent files.
- **Capture** fits the device, recording options and unfinished sessions into one section.
- **Run tests** lists your suites with their latest result, the latest run and open issues. It replaces the Tests button in the main toolbar.

## Tests

The Tests workspace is rebuilt on tiles: Suites, Library, Activity, Workspace, Execution, Details and Evidence. It has Standard and Workspace focus presets, and the layout is remembered.

- Selecting a case shows its details, steps and last run beside the list. A "Last 6 runs" column shows each case's recent results.
- A suite's new **Health** tab shows pass rate, median duration, flaky cases and open issues over the last 12 runs, a case-by-run grid, and where the latest failed run first failed.
- **Re-run failed** appears on a suite after a run with failures.
- The edition banner is replaced by a slim notice and a usage meter.

## Diagrams

The diagram workspace's Messages, Lifelines, Artifacts and Preview are tiles, remembered per diagram. You can now start from an empty diagram and add lifelines and messages by hand.

## Capture

Capture now offers H.264 and AV1. A saved H.265 setting switches to H.264 and keeps your other capture settings. Existing H.265 recordings still play.
