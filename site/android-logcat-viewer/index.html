Features / Android logcat viewer

A focused Android logcat viewer.

Open a logcat file, preserve its shape, and investigate what happened around the line that matters. Indagium is a desktop viewer for Android logs on macOS, Windows, and Linux.

The viewer

Keep the whole trace available.

The viewer brings reading, navigation, and investigation into one workspace so a useful clue does not disappear into a copied snippet.

01

Open large logs

Indagium supports multi-gigabyte logs, with optional split-on-open for the biggest files.

02

See structure

Use a minimap, thread map, time deltas, stack-trace folding, and custom issue rules while you read.

03

Stay in motion

Open multiple tabs, compare two tabs side by side, or tail a file with the current filter already applied.

04

Fold repetition

Collapse recurring regions into colored, nestable groups, or manually fold an explicit range.
